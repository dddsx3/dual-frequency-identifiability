## 5. What changed in this revision

The earlier version of this package reported a threshold of 0.159/0.375
mm (kg m^-3)^-1 at 10 % and a width floor of 0.645--1.111 m. Those values came
from three defects that this revision removes, and the record is kept here
because a reader comparing versions will notice the change:

1. the density term of the posterior variance was computed from
   `1/(1' R^-1 1) + a`, which is valid only when density is the sole nuisance;
   the correct first term uses the whole covariance minus the density group, and
   the difference is up to 46 % of `sigma^2`;
2. the sign of `dz/drho_i` was taken from the inverse map (`+h/Delta rho`)
   instead of the forward map (`-h/Delta rho`), which inverted the helpful
   direction of the differential sensitivity;
3. the response was evaluated by inverting the full covariance at every `eta`
   and the thresholds were read off a coarse grid, so the edges were not
   reproducible from the archived code.

Appendix A now contains the derivations, and `check_package.py` binds every
headline number in the manuscript to the CSV cell that produced it.

## 6. The one script that needs an archive

`code/feasibility_anchor.py` audits the read-only holdings described in
Section 5 (CCI L3C, CS2SMOS v206, ATL20 V005, IceBird, PIOMAS). Those holdings
are not redistributed. To re-run it:

```
python feasibility_anchor.py --data-root /path/to/holdings --atl20 /path/to/atl20
```

It requires `h5py` and `netCDF4`. Its output for the holdings audited in the
paper is committed as `out/feasibility_anchor.json`; the script itself does not
carry a copy of any threshold, it reads them from `out/p5_window.csv`, because a
hard-coded copy is how a superseded number survived into the earlier version.
